# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to communicate clearly, respectfully, and in good
faith.  Technical review should focus on the statement, hypotheses, proof
term, reproducibility, and documented scope.  Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, and deliberate misrepresentation of an unproved result are
not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove comments or contributions that violate this
standard and may request a change of communication channel when a discussion
cannot remain constructive.
